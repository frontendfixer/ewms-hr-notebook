# EWMS UX Specification

## Principles

- **Action First** — "I worked Sunday", not "Create CompOffCredit"
- **Dashboard First** — Home Feed command center
- **Timeline First** — chronological cards, not tables
- **Mobile First** — FAB, bottom nav, cards; desktop gets a sidebar

## Navigation

### Mobile bottom nav

Fixed bottom bar (`lg:hidden`):

| Tab | Route |
|-----|-------|
| Home | `/home` |
| Calendar | `/calendar` |
| Timeline | `/timeline` |
| Claims | `/claims` |
| More | `/more` |

On mobile the tabs are a floating, blurred dock pinned above the safe area; the active tab gets a tinted pill behind its icon. "More" is active on `/more`, `/leaves`, `/settings`, `/profile`, `/reports` and `/search`.

### Desktop sidebar

Sticky left column, one viewport tall (`lg:` breakpoint), so it stays put while the page scrolls. The layout wrapper uses `overflow-x-clip`: `overflow-x-hidden` would make it a scroll container and break sticky positioning.

- **Brand:** the logo tile (train mark on a blue gradient), name, tagline. On phones, where the sidebar is hidden, the same tile sits at the left of the top bar and links home. The logo also appears on the login and onboarding screens, the More page footer, the favicons, the Apple touch icon and the PWA icons (one source: `src/components/brand/logo.tsx`; assets regenerated by `scripts/generate-brand-assets.sh`).
- **Menu:** Home, Calendar, Timeline, Claims. The active item is tinted with an accent bar.
- **Manage:** Leaves, Reports, Settings.
- **Footer:** the signed-in user (avatar, name, email), linking to Profile.

### FAB (Quick Add)

Fixed bottom-right. Expands to:

1. **Take Leave** → `/add/leave`
2. **Add CR** → `/add/cr` (holiday/Sunday work)
3. **Night Duty** → `/add/night-duty`
4. **Travel** → `/add/travel`
5. **Payment** → `/add/payment` (redirects to Claims)

Domain-colored icons via `DOMAIN_STYLES`. No attachment action.

### Command palette

Sticky header search bar. Opens modal on click or `⌘K` / `/`. Debounced redirect to `/search?q=...` after 2+ characters.

### More page (mobile hub)

A profile card (avatar, name, designation) linking to Profile, then one grouped list: Leaves & CR (with the CL / LAP / LHAP / CR balances as its subtitle), Reports, Settings. Sign out sits in its own row. Search is the header bar on every page, so it isn't repeated here. There are no record-type shortcuts: the Timeline is the only list of entries.

### Sign-in and sessions

- **Login screen** (`/login`) — mobile first: brand mark, one line on what the app does, four feature rows, and a full-width "Sign in with Google" button at the bottom where the thumb is. Errors from Google (cancelled, expired link) are worded plainly; "You've been signed out" shows after a deliberate sign-out. Already signed in? `/login` goes straight to the app.
- **Stay signed in** — a session lasts a year and slides forward: while the app is open it pings `/api/session/keep-alive` (at most every 6 hours, and when the tab becomes visible), which renews the session and its cookie. Someone who opens the app now and then is never signed out. Sign out from More ends the session on purpose.
- **If a session lapses anyway** — the app remembers the signed-in email on the device, only as a hint for Google (it is never shown on screen, and it is not a credential). The login page then offers "Continue with Google" and, once per tab, starts Google sign-in by itself with that email as a hint; if the user is still signed in to Google nothing is shown and they are back in the app. After a deliberate sign-out, an error, or when access is revoked, the hint is cleared and it does not auto-start. "Use a different Google account" clears it and opens Google's account chooser.
- **Revoked access** — Google is asked for a refresh token. About once a day the server uses it to confirm the user still allows the app. A refusal is only recorded (Google or the network may be down, or the app's credentials misconfigured); retries are a day apart, and the user's sessions are ended only when refusals have gone on for a week from the first one. Users who signed in before this existed have no refresh token: their next sign-in asks Google for consent once to get one.
- **Sign out** — More has "Sign out" (this device) and "Sign out of all devices" (every session of the account).
- Page loads only read the session; the keep-alive route is the one place that renews it, so the database expiry and the cookie never drift apart. Keep the Google consent screen "In production": in "Testing" Google expires refresh tokens after 7 days.
- Google's tokens are encrypted in the database.

## Screens

### Home (`/home`)

Compact, one-and-a-half screens on a phone. Top to bottom:

1. **Header** — greeting, today's date and the next holiday with a countdown.
2. **Balances** — one card: CR / CL / LAP / LHAP in a row, plus a "Claims due ₹" row.
3. **Needs attention** — reminders (pay setup, CR expiring ≤14 days, bills awaiting payment or not yet billed, short-paid claims). Top two show; the rest sit behind "Show N more". Only urgent items (CR ≤7 days, pay setup) get a solid button.
4. **Summary** — one card with a This month / This year toggle: night duty, leave, travel and CR counts, then pending claims (month) or Sundays worked, night-duty average and top destination (year).
5. **Recent activity** — latest three entries (five on wider screens) with domain icons. A new user sees quick-add shortcuts here instead.

`loading.tsx` shows a skeleton of the same shape.

### Calendar (`/calendar`)

Month grid with domain-colored dots. Tap a day to see events; link to event detail.

### Timeline (`/timeline`)

Compact rows grouped by month, one card per month (about 64px per row on mobile, vs ~90px before).

- **Header** — entry count with money / leave days / CR totals for what is listed, and an All / Month / Year switch. Month and Year cover the whole period, so booked future leave shows.
- **Chips** — All, CR, Leave, Night, TA, Pending. Type is `?filter=`, range is `?period=` (`?filter=month` from older links still works). Pending means night duty / travel whose monthly bill is not yet Paid.
- **Month header** — month name and that month's totals (claim ₹, leave days, CR earned).
- **Row** — date tile (day + weekday) tinted by domain; headline; one line of facts; the user's remark if any. Right side: the key figure and a status pill.
  - Night duty: time window, night hours · ₹ · claim status.
  - Travel: route, times, claim %, purpose · ₹ · claim status.
  - Leave: type, date range, half day, days not counted · days charged.
  - CR: day worked (Sunday / holiday name), expiry or the leave that used it · +CR and Available / N d left / Used / Expired.
- **Row menu (⋮)** — Edit (when not locked) and Delete. Tapping the row opens the detail page.
- Shows the latest 100 entries; "Show older entries" adds 100 more (max 500).
- `HOLIDAY_WORK_RECORDED` events are hidden from lists (the paired CR credit is shown instead).
- The same rows are used by search results and a month's entries on the claim page.

`loading.tsx` shows a skeleton of the same shape.

### Event detail (`/timeline/[eventId]`)

Domain header, remarks, claim status vertical stepper (night duty / travel), ledger impact, related events (via `correlationId`), void action.

### Claims (`/claims`)

Night duty and travel are billed monthly, one bill per type per month. The page is built for chasing money on a phone.

- **Overview card** — what is **due to you**, a stacked bar of where it is stuck (Unbilled / Billed / Passed / Short-paid, with amounts), and the calendar year so far: received of claimed, split into night duty and travel.
- **Open / Paid / All** switch with counts (`?view=paid|all`; Open is the default). Open means not paid in full, including bills paid short.
- **Months** — newest first. A month header shows what was claimed and what is still due; under it, one row per bill (night duty, then travel).
- **Bill row** — icon, type and entry count; entry date range, bill number, how long it has been waiting ("Billed 12 days ago", "Passed today"), what was received or the short-paid amount; on the right the amount still due (or the total once paid) and a status pill. The whole row opens the bill.

### Claim detail (`/claims/[settlementId]`)

- **Header** — type, month, entry count and date range, status pill (red "Short-paid" when a paid bill is below the claim).
- **Money card** — Claimed / Received / Due (or Short by), the payment history (date, reference, amount), a link to that month's report statement, and a link to the other bill of the same month with its status.
- **Status stepper** — Entries recorded, Bill Submitted (bill number optional), Passed, Paid (amount received, date, reference), each with its date and note; the next step's form opens in place; Undo last step.
- **Entries** — the month's night duty or travel rows, each with its own status.

`loading.tsx` shows a skeleton of the same shape.

### Reports (`/reports`)

One month at a time, built for a phone (no sideways-scrolling tables).

- **Month picker** — previous / next arrows and a tap-to-pick month label that applies at once (browsers without a month picker show the label only; the arrows and the year chart still navigate). "Today" jumps back to the current month in India.
- **Summary card** — claims in the month with the change against the previous month, a night duty / travel split bar, money status (not billed yet / received / due, "Short-paid" when a bill was paid below the claim, "All paid"), CR earned and used, leave days, and today's CL / LAP / LHAP / CR balances.
- **Four sections** — Night duty, Travelling allowance, CR earned, Leave taken. Each is a card of two-line rows (night duty: time window, night hours, paid hours, rate, DA %; travel: route, date, times, claim % of the daily rate, purpose; CR: day worked, earned / used, valid till, status; leave: type, range, days) with your remark, the section total, a CSV download, and a bill-status footer with a link to the bill. A section with nothing in it collapses to one line.
- **Financial year card** — year-to-date NDA, TA, leave days and CR earned, and a 12-bar April to March chart of claims; each bar opens that month.
- **Print / PDF** keeps the statement header, summary, sections and year totals; controls and the chart are hidden.
- **Insights** (`/reports/insights`) — calendar-year counts (Sundays worked, CR credits, night duties, journeys, leave entries), pending claims for the month and the most visited destination.

`loading.tsx` shows a skeleton of the same shape.

### Settings (`/settings`)

Theme toggle (light / dark / system). Pay settings form: basic pay, DA %, TA base, CR expiry days with live DA preview. Link to holidays management.

### Onboarding (`/onboarding`)

4 steps: Welcome → Profile (name, personnel no., designation, department) → Opening leave balances (CL, LAP, LHAP) → Done.

## Key Flows

### 1. Onboarding → Home Feed

New user signs in → onboarding wizard sets profile and opening balances → redirected to Home.

### 2. Leave with FIFO CR

Take Leave wizard → select SPECIAL_CL → app recommends oldest CR credits (FIFO) → records `LEAVE_RECORDED` + `CR_CONSUMED` with shared `correlationId`.

### 3. Claim lifecycle

Record night duty or travel (starts as DRAFT) → open event detail → vertical stepper: Mark as Bill Submitted → Passed → Paid. Each transition appends a child event (`BILL_SUBMITTED`, `CLAIM_PASSED`, `PAYMENT_RECEIVED`).

### 4. Void / delete

Event detail → delete → appends `EVENT_VOIDED` with compensating ledger reversals. Never edits original event body.

## Design Tokens

- Spacing: 8px grid
- Radius: `0.75rem` (12px) for cards, sheets, and nav items
- Status: emerald=success, amber=pending/warning, blue=progress/info, red=expired/critical
- Domain colors: emerald=CR, rose=Leave, blue=Night Duty, amber=Travel, muted=Meta
